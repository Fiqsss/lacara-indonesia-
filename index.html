About PT Lacara Food Indonesia

PT LACARA FOOD INDONESIA is a trusted international trading company based in Pati, Central Java, Indonesia.

Inspired by Indonesia’s rich natural resources, we provide a carefully selected range of products sourced from experienced farmers, fishermen, producers, and trusted supply partners across the Indonesian archipelago, particularly from Java, Sumatra, and Kalimantan.

Every product we offer undergoes a careful selection process with a strong emphasis on quality, consistency, and reliability. From sourcing and sorting to handling, packing, and shipment preparation, we apply high standards throughout each stage to help maintain product quality and ensure that products arrive at our buyers in proper condition.

For our Seafood & Marine Products, we place particular attention on proper handling, storage, and packaging to help preserve freshness and product quality throughout the distribution process. For our Food Products & Condiments, we focus on carefully selected raw materials, consistent production processes, and appropriate packaging to support the requirements of both domestic and international markets.

At PT LACARA FOOD INDONESIA, international trade is not only about delivering products. It is also about building trust, maintaining strong business relationships, and creating long-term partnerships.

Guided by our core values of Quality, Integrity, and Reliability, we are committed to being a dependable trading partner in bringing selected Indonesian products to distributors, importers, businesses, and consumers across international markets.
